# Legends of the Forgotten Realm

A text-based fantasy RPG game developed using Python. Create your character, explore mysterious regions, battle enemies and powerful bosses, collect weapons and armour, and become the hero who saves the kingdom of Eldoria.

## About the Game

The kingdom of Eldoria has fallen into darkness. Dangerous monsters have taken over villages, forests, caves, and ancient ruins. Powerful bosses guard the path to new regions, and the Ancient Demon King threatens the entire kingdom.

Your mission is to grow stronger, defeat enemies and bosses, explore the world, and reach Level 100 to challenge the Ancient Demon King.

## Features
- **Character Creation:** Choose your character name and class.
- **Multiple Character Classes:** Play as a Warrior, Mage, Archer, or Assassin.
- **Combat System:** Battle enemies using attacks, defence, and other available actions.
- **Level Progression:** Gain experience points and improve your character's abilities.
- **Weapons and Armour:** Collect equipment to increase your combat strength.
- **Potions:** Use available potions to support your character.
- **World Map:** View regions and travel to accessible locations.
- **Boss Battles:** Fight powerful bosses as you progress through the game.
- **Loot System:** Earn gold and collect items from defeated enemies.
- **Save and Load:** Save your progress and continue your adventure, once implemented.
- **Character Statistics:** View your character's level, health, mana, equipment, and other statistics.

## Character Classes

      
- **Warrior :**	A strong fighter with high health and defence.
- **Mage :**	A magic user with high mana and powerful attacks.
- **Archer :**	A ranged fighter with a good critical-hit chance.
- **Assassin :**	A fast, offensive fighter with a high critical-hit chance.

## How to Play
- Run main.py.
- Select New Game from the main menu.
- Enter your character name.
- Choose a character class.
- Explore the world and encounter enemies.
- Fight enemies to earn experience points and gold.
- Collect equipment and improve your character.
- Unlock new regions by meeting the game's progression requirements.
- Defeat the bosses guarding your journey.
- Reach Level 100 and defeat the Ancient Demon King to complete your adventure.

## Game Objective
The primary objective is to develop a powerful character, explore the world of Eldoria, defeat progressively stronger enemies and bosses, and ultimately defeat the Ancient Demon King
